Android page: Settings > Actions

Actions

Tap, hold, or fling in an outer region to use its assigned action. The center button has its own assignments. Choose NONE when a control should do nothing.

Feedback

Vibration

Enable vibration feedback when Scrool responds to an action.

Touchpad Tap

Choose an action for a quick touch in each outer area.

Tap top

Action for a quick touch in the top area.

Tap bottom

Action for a quick touch in the bottom area.

Tap left

Action for a quick touch in the left area.

Tap right

Action for a quick touch in the right area.

Touchpad Long Press

Long press top

Action for holding in the top area.

Long press bottom

Action for holding in the bottom area.

Long press left

Action for holding in the left area.

Long press right

Action for holding in the right area.

Choose an action for each quick flick in the four directions.

Fling up

Fling down

Action for a flick downward.

Fling left

Action for a flick to the left.

Fling right

Action for a flick to the right.

Center button fling right

Cursor Dwell Settings

A dwell happens when the cursor stays still for the configured time.

Dwell action

Choose what happens when the cursor stays still long enough.

Dwell trigger duration

Choose how long the cursor must stay still before the dwell action starts.

Dwell repeat distance

Choose how far the cursor must move before the same dwell action can happen again.

Miscellaneous

Long press trigger duration

Choose how long you must hold the pad before a long-press action starts.

Timed hide duration

Choose how long the pad stays hidden after choosing TIMED HIDE.

Enable gesture reset

Enable the Android Accessibility touch fix for more reliable touch actions on supported Android versions.

Available actions

Choose one of these actions for a touchpad area, center-button gesture, or dwell.

NONE

No action assigned.

TAP

Send a tap gesture.

LONG PRESS

Send a long-press gesture.

DRAG

Drag across the screen.

SWIPE

Send a swipe gesture.

CHARGED SWIPE

Send a longer swipe gesture.

PINCH

Pinch to zoom out.

SPREAD

Spread to zoom in.

BACK

Navigate back.

HOME

Return to the home screen.

RECENTS

Show recent apps.

APP DRAWER

Open the apps list.

NOTIFICATIONS

Open the notification shade.

QUICK SETTINGS

Open quick settings.

SCREENSHOT

Capture the current screen.

HIDE TOUCHPAD

Hide the virtual touchpad.

TIMED HIDE

Hide the touchpad for a limited time.

RESIZE TOUCHPAD

Adjust the touchpad size.

D-PAD UP

Send a directional pad up control.

D-PAD DOWN

Send a directional pad down control.

D-PAD LEFT

Send a directional pad left control.

D-PAD RIGHT

Send a directional pad right control.

Some actions depend on the Android version and may not appear on every phone.
