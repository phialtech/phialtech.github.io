Android page: Settings > Swipe

Swipe

In a range editor, Center is a touch near the middle of the pad and Edge is a touch near its outside edge. A range lets the gesture change gradually across the pad.

Swipe

Swipe duration and distance

Set how quickly and how far a swipe moves. Center and Edge values control the small and large ends of the range.

Swipe directions

Allow movement in any direction or restrict swipes to straight up, down, left, and right.

Omnidirectional

Allow a swipe to travel in any direction.

Cardinal

Limit swipes to up, down, left, and right.

Invert X

Reverse left and right movement for horizontal swipes.

Invert Y

Reverse up and down movement for vertical swipes.

Pinch and Spread

Pinch or spread duration

Choose how quickly small and large zoom gestures happen.

Per-finger pinch or spread distance

Choose how far each generated finger stroke moves for small and large zoom gestures.

Finger separation offset

Choose how far apart the two fingers start from the cursor.
