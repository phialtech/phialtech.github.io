Scrool turns a small floating touchpad into a cursor for your phone. Tap, scroll, swipe, and use Android actions—all from a comfortable spot.

Get it on Google Play

On-screen touchpad

Touchpad wherever you need it.

The touchpad floats over your apps, so you can place it where it’s easiest to reach. Keep it nearby while using your usual apps.

Cursor control

Move the cursor anywhere.

Slide on the touchpad to move the cursor across the screen, then tap to interact. Your hand can stay in a comfortable position while the cursor reaches farther.

Scrolling and gestures

Scroll and swipe from the pad.

Tap to click, or swipe and fling on the touchpad to scroll through pages, feeds, and wide content. Use gestures like pinch and spread to zoom where supported.

Appearance

Customize your touchpad.

Adjust the touchpad and cursor size, color, and transparency. Choose an appearance that stays visible without covering too much of your screen.

Assignable by region

Set actions by touchpad region.

Give each touchpad region its own tap, hold, and fling actions. Set up the controls to match the way you use your phone.

App-specific behavior

Customize controls by app.

Choose different touchpad actions and behavior for selected apps. Scrool applies those settings when you use each app and returns to your default settings elsewhere.
